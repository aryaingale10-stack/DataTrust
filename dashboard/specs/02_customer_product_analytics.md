# Page 2 — Customer & Product Analytics

## Purpose

The Customer & Product Analytics page provides a deeper view of who generates NovaRetail's revenue and which products drive business performance.

The page is designed to answer five analytical questions:

1. Which products and product categories generate the most revenue and profit?
2. Which products have the strongest and weakest profitability?
3. Who are NovaRetail's highest-value customers?
4. How do repeat and one-time customers differ in their contribution to the business?
5. Which customer and product segments should analysts investigate further?

## Primary Data Sources

- `pbi_product_performance`
- `pbi_customer_performance`

## Supporting Analytical Work

The page is supported by the advanced SQL analyses developed for:

- Product and category profitability
- Customer repeat-purchase behavior
- RFM customer segmentation
- Top products within category

## Page Layout

The page will contain four main sections:

1. Customer Summary
2. Customer Value Analysis
3. Product & Category Performance
4. Profitability Analysis

## Product & Category Performance

### Primary Visual — Top Products by Net Sales

**Visual Type:** Horizontal Bar Chart

**Category:**
- `product_name`

**Primary Value:**
- `net_sales`

**Default Display:**
- Top 10 products by `net_sales`

**Tooltip Fields:**
- `product_id`
- `category`
- `subcategory`
- `brand`
- `total_orders`
- `total_customers`
- `units_sold`
- `gross_profit`
- `gross_margin_pct`
- `overall_revenue_rank`
- `category_revenue_rank`

### Business Question

Which products generate the most revenue, and how do their sales volume and profitability compare?

### Product Drill-Down Hierarchy

The product analysis should support exploration through:

`category` → `subcategory` → `product_name`

The `brand` field should be available as an additional slicer or filter.

### Ranking

- `overall_revenue_rank` identifies each product's revenue position across the full product portfolio.
- `category_revenue_rank` identifies each product's revenue position within its category.

The primary chart should be sorted by `net_sales` in descending order.

## Repeat vs. One-Time Customer Analysis

### Primary Visual — Revenue Contribution by Customer Type

**Visual Type:** Donut Chart

**Legend:**
- `customer_type`

**Value:**
- `lifetime_revenue`

**Tooltip Fields:**
- Customer count
- Total orders
- Lifetime profit
- Revenue share

### Business Question

How much of NovaRetail's revenue is generated by repeat customers compared with one-time customers?

### Current Customer Snapshot

| Customer Type | Customers | Customer Share | Total Orders | Revenue | Revenue Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Repeat Customer | 33,082 | 75.04% | 103,533 | $153.52M | 90.20% |
| One-Time Customer | 11,006 | 24.96% | 11,006 | $16.69M | 9.80% |

### Analytical Insight

Repeat customers represent approximately 75% of customers but generate approximately 90% of total revenue in the trusted sales dataset.

This visual highlights the importance of repeat purchasing behavior without implying that repeat purchasing itself caused the higher revenue contribution.

## Category Revenue & Profitability Analysis

### Primary Visual — Category Revenue and Gross Margin

**Visual Type:** Line and Clustered Column Chart

**X-Axis:**
- `category`

**Column Value:**
- `net_sales`

**Line Value:**
- `gross_margin_pct`

**Tooltip Fields:**
- Product count
- `units_sold`
- `gross_profit`
- `total_cost`

### Business Question

Which product categories generate the most revenue, and how does their profitability compare?

### Current Category Snapshot

| Category | Net Sales | Gross Profit | Gross Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Electronics | $62.17M | $12.49M | 20.09% |
| Sports & Outdoors | $39.06M | $9.50M | 24.32% |
| Home & Kitchen | $34.52M | $8.44M | 24.46% |
| Office Supplies | $21.66M | $5.93M | 27.39% |
| Clothing | $12.80M | $4.91M | 38.34% |

### Analytical Interpretation

Electronics currently contributes the highest net sales among the five categories.

Clothing generates substantially less revenue but has the highest gross margin percentage.

Displaying revenue and margin together helps distinguish high-revenue categories from high-margin categories rather than treating sales volume alone as product performance.

## Page Filters and Slicers

The Customer & Product Analytics page will provide the following interactive filters.

### Product Filters

- `category`
- `subcategory`
- `brand`

These filters apply to the product performance analysis.

### Customer Filters

- `customer_type`
- `region`
- `state`

These filters apply to the customer performance analysis.

### Interaction Behavior

Product filters should update:

- Top Products by Net Sales
- Category Revenue & Gross Margin
- Product-level profitability analysis

Customer filters should update:

- Top Customers by Lifetime Revenue
- Repeat vs. One-Time Customer analysis
- Customer-level performance analysis

### Filter Design

Use dropdown slicers where the number of possible values is large, particularly:

- `subcategory`
- `brand`
- `state`

Customer Type and Category may use compact button-style slicers because they contain relatively few values.

### Important Modeling Note

`pbi_product_performance` and `pbi_customer_performance` are separate aggregated reporting datasets.

Cross-filtering between customer-level and product-level visuals should not be assumed unless the final Power BI semantic model provides a valid relationship or uses the underlying star-schema tables.

Filters must only affect visuals supported by their actual data relationships.
